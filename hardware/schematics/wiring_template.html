Wiring map

Every wire in the first box, from the Pi Zero 2 W's 40-pin header to each part's terminals. There are no status LEDs: Record glows dim blue when the box is ready and blinks blue when it is not. Red is only ever the mic. One wire per header pin. Where a signal reaches two parts, it is chained at the part, never doubled at the header. Hover a wire to trace it, or pick a part to see only its wires.

Wire colours

The ten colours of the ribbon cable in hand. Red is 5 V and black is ground everywhere; the two buttons are wired alike; each I²S clock keeps its colour from the header through the amp to the mic. The count is how many wires of each colour to cut.

No red light, no mic

Pin 11 (GPIO 17) goes to the record button's R tab first. A second wire from that same tab powers the mic's VDD. A broken wire can only leave the mic unpowered, never powered with the red light dark.

One audio interface, two parts

BCLK (pin 12) and LRCLK (pin 35) go to the amp. Short wires chain them from the amp's pads to the mic's SCK and WS. The mic's data comes back on its own pin, 38.

Chains are soldered

Both wires into the same tab or pad, heat-shrink over it. No two Dupont housings on one pin, anywhere. On each button C− (the LED's cathode) is linked to one gold switch tab; the mic's L/R is joined to its GND. All at the part.
